### CHEM-000425: The nickel hydride behind additive-switched silylene–Ni hydrosilylation
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Identify the active Ni species generated by nBuLi from the bis(silylene)-pincer Ni(II) chloride precursor. Determine whether intact pincer-bound Ni–H accounts for Markovnikov selectivity.
- **Status:** A 2026 paper reports opposite regioselectivity with nBuLi and KOtBu. It suggests a nickel hydride as the active catalyst under the former conditions without establishing its structure and abundance.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Distinguishes ligand-controlled selectivity from additive-induced catalyst reconstruction.
- **Key works:** Z. Yang et al. (2026), “Additive-controlled selective hydrosilylation of aryl alkenes catalyzed by bis(silylene) pincer nickel(II) chloride,” [DOI: 10.1039/D5NJ03998C](https://doi.org/10.1039/D5NJ03998C).
- **Related:** Control theory—inferring hidden system changes from a switched response.
